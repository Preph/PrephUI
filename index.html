Enhance Without Bloat

Modern WoW has a lot built-in and just needs some nudges. PrephUI utilizes built-in secure templates and alleviates work to built-ins, to gather the most performance while adding the utmost needed quality of life and visual fidelity.

Our Philosophy

Inspired by the comprehensive QoL of other Interface Replacements and smart AddOns, PrephUI takes the next logical step to utilize built-ins and combine more QoL, with lesser focus on just the UI iteslf.

Instead of completely reconstructing the UI from scratch and dragging along tens of heavy libraries, we aim to be as slim as possible. By leveraging native Blizzard code and secure templates, we deliver a clean, fast, and highly functional interface that feels like an evolution of the default game rather than a total replacement.

  • Minimal external library dependencies.
  • Built on top of native secure templates and modern built-ins.
  • And a focus on not just Interface related Quality of Life we also ship features like an Auto-Inviter with temporary kewywords and alike.

Preph's Damage Meter

A damage meter that reads Blizzard's built-in combat data, so it stays light and keeps working in combat. Up to five windows, ten meter types, a death recap on every death row, and fights that survive a reload.

Modern Style

What it tracks

Every window shows one meter type. Change it from the window title or with a right-click.

Output

Damage Done, Healing Done and Absorbs. Bars show per-second values next to the total, and your own bar stays pinned when you scroll past it.

Intake

Damage Taken, Avoidable Damage Taken and Enemy Damage Taken. Useful for seeing who ate the mechanic and what your group did to each mob.

Utility

Interrupts, Dispels, Deaths and Threat. Threat is live only: it follows your target, focus or boss units and shows accumulated threat and percent.

Hover for the breakdown

Mouse over any bar to see what is behind the number. What you see depends on the meter type:

  • Damage and healing: abilities, plus the targets hit or healed.
  • Damage taken: which abilities hurt, and which mobs or players dealt it.
  • Interrupts and dispels: which abilities were stopped or removed.
  • Absorbs: shield abilities and who got shielded.

Blizzard hides some values while you are in combat. Ranks and full target lists fill in a few seconds after the fight ends, and the tooltip tells you when something isn't available yet.

Death recap

Hover a row in the Deaths meter to see the last hits before that player died, oldest first, with the killing blow highlighted.

  • Each hit shows its ability, source, amount and how many seconds before death it landed.
  • A health bar per hit shows how quickly they dropped.
  • Absorbed damage and overkill are listed on the hit they belong to.
  • Healing they received is inferred from health changes and shown in green.
  • A summary line gives hit count, damage taken and top damage sources.
  • Hunters using Feign Death are filtered out, so they don't show up as real deaths (optional).

Two looks

Modern is flat bars on a dark window. WoW Forever uses a bronze frame edge, a dark brown title bar, the classic bar texture and outlined text. It is one checkbox in the settings and applies to all windows at once.

Bar texture and font can be changed separately if you have LibSharedMedia loaded.

Windows and fights

  • 1 to 5 windows. Each has its own meter type and its own fight. A new second window starts on healing, a third on damage taken.
  • Snapping. Drag a window next to another and it offers to anchor, either keeping its size or matching the neighbour.
  • Current, Overall and past fights. Pick from the fight button in the window header. Up to 25 recent fights are listed.
  • Survives /reload. The last 5 fights and Overall are saved, with ability details, and show up under "Saved".
  • Mythic+. Data can reset when a key starts, so Overall covers exactly that key.
  • Sync option. Pick a fight in one window and every window follows.

Settings

Open with /pdm config.

  • Sliders: windows (1-5), bar height, bar spacing, font size, background opacity, scale, and refresh rate in combat (0.2 to 5 seconds).
  • Fonts and textures from LibSharedMedia, previewed in the picker.
  • Toggles: class and spec icons, per-second values, pin my bar, lock windows, padlock only on mouseover.
  • Behaviour: hide Blizzard's own meter, jump to Current on the next pull, reset on keystone start, sync fight selection.
  • Reset for window layout or for all data, plus an in-game changelog.

Works with PrephUI

Install it alongside PrephUI and the windows become movable elements in PrephUI's edit mode, so you can anchor them to your other frames. Without PrephUI it runs as a normal standalone addon.

Text is localized, and Chinese and Russian clients get a font that can display their characters.

Slash commands

/pdmShow or hide the meter
/pdm configOpen settings
/pdm windows 3Set the number of windows (1-5)
/pdm lock, unlockLock or unlock position and size
/pdm layoutReset window positions
/pdm resetClear all data (asks first)
